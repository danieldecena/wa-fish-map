There is no docs/SKELETON.md: the "skeleton" is the two published maps' working copies.

## Brainstorm

**A. River map, Typical season: zoomed-in pins all say "Good".** Every pin carries "Name · Good" or a bare "Good", so the text repeats the color 60 times and crowds the names out.
- Option 1: name-only labels everywhere, rating in color plus the spoken label. Rejected: the page has a stated rule that color is never the only carrier of a rating (see `tShort` comment).
- Option 2 (chosen): **exceptions only**. Work out the majority rating among the sites in view. Pins with the majority rating show the name only. Pins that differ show "Name · Rating", and a pin with no room for its name shows the rating word. The card states the majority in words ("Unlabeled pins in view: Good"), so the rating is still carried by text.
- Option 3: hide labels below a zoom threshold. Rejected because it hides names, which are the useful part.

**B. Spawning map: a group takes its best member's color.** A "5" group colored red says all five are at peak when only one may be. The river map already solved this with neutral groups and a ring showing the rating mix.
- Option 1 (chosen): **port the river map's ring.** A group with mixed statuses gets a neutral fill plus a ring of shares. A group whose members all agree keeps the solid color.
- Option 2: color by majority. Rejected because it still claims one color for mixed members.

**C. Spawning map: month buttons are about 28px wide on phones.**
- Option 1 (chosen): **drag across the ribbon to change months** (pointer scrub), keeping tap and arrow keys. The whole 330px ribbon becomes the target.
- Option 2: a range slider. Rejected because it loses the per-month bars, which are the page's main visual.

## Acceptance criteria per item
**1 (river):**
- In Typical season, zoomed in, pins whose rating equals the in-view majority show the site name only. Pins that differ show "Name · Rating". A pin with no room for its name falls back to the rating word **only when it differs from the majority**; otherwise it shows no label.
- The card shows one line of text naming the majority, e.g. "Unlabeled pins in view: Good". It updates on pan and zoom and is hidden when no site in view is rated.
- Recent reports mode is unchanged.
- Spoken pin labels (`aria-label`) still include the rating for every pin.
- No console errors. Renders at 1440×900 light and 390×844 dark.

**2 (spawn):**
- A mixed group draws a neutral fill (a new `--cluster` token, light #ffffff and dark #2c2c2e) with a ring of arcs sized by each status's share: peak, active, starting or ending, quiet, species not listed. A group whose members all share one status stays solid in that color.
- The group's spoken label lists the counts, e.g. "6 sites close together: 2 peak, 3 active, 1 quiet. Zoom in."
- The legend gains "Group: ring shows the mix".
- Pressing on the month ribbon and dragging horizontally changes the month live. Taps and arrow keys still work. It doesn't scroll the page or pan the map.
- No console errors, no horizontal overflow. Renders at 1440×900 light and 390×844 dark.
