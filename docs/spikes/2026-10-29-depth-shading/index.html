Depth shading from open bathymetry (spike)

Three test areas. Left: the Depth layer as published today (NOAA ENC contours, depth-data.js, used unchanged). Middle: colour relief and smoothed contours baked from NOAA NCEI CUDEM, 512 px wide per area. Right: both together. Everything is local, no network. A guess from a chart and a model, never for navigation.

Feet below low tide

Wheel or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset; the three panels of an area move together. Lines stay sharp because they are vectors; the shading is a raster and softens or blocks up as you zoom, which is the point of this test.

Data: NOAA NCEI, Continuously Updated Digital Elevation Model (CUDEM) 1/9 arc-second tiles, not subject to copyright in the US; NOAA Office of Coast Survey ENC contours. CUDEM heights are metres on NAVD88, shifted here to feet below MLLW with the nearest tide station's separation. Details and the licence table are in the README beside this page.
