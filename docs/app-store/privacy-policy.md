# WAFishMap — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-16

WAFishMap (“the App”) is a Washington State fishing map that shows boat launches, piers, rivers, and editorial seasonal guidance. This policy describes what the App accesses and what it does **not** do.

## Data the App accesses on your device

| Data | When | Why | Leaves the device? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Location** (when in use) | After you grant permission | Center the map near you and find nearby launches | No. Location is used on-device only. |
| **Network** | While online | Load WDFW map data, NOAA tides/buoys, USGS river flow, and Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the plan time | Yes — only to those providers for the request you triggered. |

The App does **not** create an account, does **not** sell or share personal data with advertisers, and does **not** use tracking across apps or websites.

## Third-party services

- **Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife (WDFW)** — public geospatial services for marine areas and access sites.
- **NOAA** — tide predictions and buoy observations.
- **USGS** — river discharge and water temperature.
- **Apple WeatherKit** — wind and precipitation for the planned date/time. Apple’s weather terms and attribution appear in the App when weather is shown.

Those services receive the coordinates or identifiers needed to answer the request (for example, a map area or a gauge id). They are not used to build a profile of you.

## Guidance vs regulation

Seasonal ratings and “what’s biting” text are **editorial timing guidance**, not fishing regulations, bag limits, or open/closed season dates. Always confirm rules in the official **Fish Washington** materials and at [wdfw.wa.gov](https://wdfw.wa.gov) before you go.

## Children

The App is intended for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
