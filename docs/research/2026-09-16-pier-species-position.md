# Pier species position: what the sources support (2026-09-16)

Research for the queued "pier habitat rings" brainstorm. Question: do sources support
drawing "at the pilings" vs "a cast out" zones per species around Puget Sound piers?

**Verdict: not as a general rule.** No WDFW source gives a pilings-vs-cast-out pattern
for any species. What exists is pier-specific or habitat-specific.

| Species | What is sourced | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market squid | Dark fringe of pier lights, night, after dark to ~midnight, cloudy/high tide best. Season moves south: Neah Bay late May, Port Angeles Jun-Aug, Edmonds Sep+, Des Moines/Tacoma late Nov-Dec, South Sound Dec-Jan. Tall lights (Edmonds) push the edge farther out. | High (WDFW) | https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/basics/squid |
| Rockfish | All rockfish released in Puget Sound; no bottomfishing deeper than 120 ft. No positional guidance. | High (WDFW) | https://wdfw.wa.gov/species-habitats/at-risk/species-recovery/rockfish |
| Dungeness / red rock crab | WDFW: piers crowded, limited opportunity. One guide: throw pots away from the dock. No piling-vs-out rule. | Low | https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/basics/recreational-crabbing-puget-sound |
| Perch, flatfish | Side-of-pier at specific piers (e.g. Les Davis: striped perch left, flounder right). Not transferable. | Low | http://northwestfishingreports.com/Articles/Details/872 |
| Sea-run cutthroat | Habitat cue (rocky bottom, eelgrass, oyster beds, near stream mouths, flood tide), not pier geometry. | Medium (WDFW blog) | https://wdfw.medium.com/the-411-on-sea-run-cutthroats-6990a5709067 |
| Salmon | Shore anglers cast jigs/buzz bombs for coho and pink; pier-end/seam detail UNSOURCED. | Low | https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/locations/marine-areas/seattle-bremerton-area |
| Lingcod, cabezon, sculpin, greenling, herring | Listed as pier catches; position UNSOURCED. | None | - |

Implications for design:
- A generic per-species ring would present folklore as fact, which the app's "guidance, not regulation" rule forbids.
- Sourced options: a squid "under the lights, at night, in season" note on marine piers by region; habitat notes (not rings) for cutthroat.
- Per-pier positional notes would need a curated per-pier tier, like `freshwater.byWaterbody`, verified pier by pier.
- Nothing may imply a rockfish keeper zone.
