# Three Friends Fishing Hole: map location

Checked 2026-10-26, for the map's Spot guides layer (`Artifacts/salmon-rivers/spots.js`).

- Point: 47.4231, -122.2643 (lat, lon)
- Source: City of Kent, "Three Friends Fishing Hole",
  https://www.kentwa.gov/departments/kent-parks/parks-places/parks-trails/three-friends-fishing-hole
  The page gives the address 19970 Russell Rd, Kent, WA 98032 and links a map pin at
  47.423104, -122.2642777. The point above is that pin rounded to four decimals.
- How it was chosen: the guide is for this park's bank access, so the pin goes on the park, not
  on the S 212th St bridge the guide mentions. The bridge is the rule boundary a little upstream
  (south); the park sits on the river near S 200th St (Wikimedia Commons, "Green River - Three
  Friends Fishing Hole.jpg", describes it as "just south of S. 200 St.").
