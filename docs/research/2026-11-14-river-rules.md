# River rules re-read for the Skagit and the Wind River, 2026-11-14

Follow-up to `2026-10-29-river-rules.md`. The Skagit and Wind River rows in `Artifacts/salmon-rivers/rules/river-rules.json` ended on Oct 31, so the web map's sheets had dropped them (a row is hidden after its `through` day). Both pages below were fetched and read in full on 2026-11-14 (page text, not a model summary).

## Skagit River, Nov 1 to 15

Source: WDFW emergency rule "Salmon seasons for Skagit and Cascade rivers extended through Nov. 15", published 2026-10-30, effective Nov 1 to 15, 2026:
`https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/regulations/emergency-rules/salmon-seasons-skagit-and-cascade-rivers-extended-through-nov-15-2026-10`

For the Skagit from the mouth to Cascade River Road (Marblemount Bridge) the notice gives: daily limit two coho, minimum size 12 inches, release all salmon other than coho; release Dolly Varden and bull trout; night closure for all species. The reason given is that coho catch share guidelines have not been reached; managers may close salmon seasons earlier if necessary.

Carried to the map (all `through` 2026-11-15):

- Coho Salmon: "Nov 1 to 15: 2 coho a day, 12 in or longer."
- Chinook Salmon (release): "Nov 1 to 15: release all salmon other than coho."
- All salmon: "Nov 1 to 15: night closure for all species. Release Dolly Varden and bull trout."

Dropped, not carried: "Bait is prohibited and single-point barbless hooks are required for salmon." That came from the Oct 15 Cascade River emergency rule (through Oct 31); the Nov 1 to 15 notice does not repeat it, so the map no longer says it. The Cascade River rule in the same notice is not on the map (the map has no Cascade sheet).

This rule ends tomorrow (Nov 15). The row drops off the sheet after that day by itself; the Skagit then shows no Rules section until the pamphlet's rule for the Skagit from Nov 16 is read and added.

## Wind River, Nov 1 to Mar 15

Source: WDFW "Fishing in Washington" pamphlet on eRegulations, Columbia Basin Rivers Special Rules: T-Z, page marked "Last Updated: October 5, 2026":
`https://www.eregulations.com/washington/fishing/columbia-basin-rivers-special-rules-t-z`

Wind River (Skamania Co.):

- Mouth to the Highway 14 bridge, Nov 1 to Mar 15: "Daily limit 3 hatchery steelhead. Release all salmon."
- Highway 14 bridge to 400 ft below the Shipherd Falls fish ladder, Nov 1 to Mar 15: "Min. size 20". Daily limit 3 hatchery steelhead. Release all salmon."
- No night closure or anti-snagging rule is listed for either stretch after Oct 31 (both end Oct 31).
- 400 ft below to 100 ft above the Shipherd Falls fish ladder: closed waters (unchanged). Columbia River Salmon and Steelhead Endorsement for anglers 15 and older (unchanged).

Carried to the map, as one stretch ("Mouth to 400 ft below the Shipherd Falls fish ladder"), Coho Salmon and Chinook Salmon rows, both release, `through` 2027-03-15: "Nov 1 to Mar 15: release all salmon. Hatchery steelhead only, 3 a day (20 in or longer above the Highway 14 bridge)." The four rows that ended Oct 31 are removed; the closed-waters and endorsement rows stay.

## Checked for anything newer

WDFW's emergency rules list (`https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/regulations/emergency-rules`, fetched 2026-11-14) was read for entries touching the report rivers. The only ones are the Skagit and Cascade notice above and "Lower Columbia River steelhead fishery update" (2026-11-13), which closes steelhead angling in the Columbia from The Dalles Dam to Highway 730 for Jan 1 to Mar 31, 2027. That is the mainstem, steelhead only, and does not change a Wind River rule. Nothing in the list names the Wind, Cowlitz, Lewis, Klickitat, Drano Lake, Kalama or Washougal.
