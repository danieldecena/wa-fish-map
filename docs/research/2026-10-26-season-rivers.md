# Season rivers: which specific waters the vague Fishing Year seasons cover

Checked 2026-10-26 (a research pass through WDFW rule text; summarised, not every reach read).
Used by `Artifacts/salmon-rivers/season-places.js`, where a place may now cite a WDFW source
instead of the season's own `where` wording.

Sources:
- [A] WSR 26-13-052, Order 26-01, 2026-2027 recreational salmon seasons, effective 2026-07-12:
  https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/law/wsr/2026/13/26-13-052.htm
- [B] WDFW emergency rules: https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/regulations/emergency-rules
- [C] WSR 26-03-004, Order 25-15, coastal steelhead and gamefish rule, effective 2026-02-08:
  https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/WSR/2026/03/26-03-004.htm

## Added to the map

- Chum (Oct 16 to Nov 30): Green (Duwamish) River, King County. [A]: Harbor Island line to
  Tukwila Blvd and Tukwila Blvd to S 212th St open Aug 20 to Dec 31, no more than 3 adult coho
  and chum combined. Emergency (Aug 2026) closed Chinook retention from Tukwila Intl Blvd to
  S 212th; chum and coho stay at 3 adults combined:
  https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/regulations/emergency-rules/green-duwamish-river-chinook-retention-closed-2026-08
  The upper reach (Auburn-Black Diamond Rd Bridge up) is closed to all fishing Sep 16 to Oct 31:
  https://wdfw.wa.gov/fishing/regulations/emergency-rules/upper-green-duwamish-river-closed-fishing-sept-16-oct-31-2026-08
  The map draws the whole river line from rivers-tap.js; the sheet text carries the reach detail.

## Not added, and why

- Other south Sound chum rivers (Nisqually, Puyallup, Kennedy, Skookum, Deschutes): chum must be
  released or salmon are closed in [A]; no 2026 emergency opening found.
- Hood Canal rivers: Dosewallips and Duckabush chum Nov 1 to Dec 15 [A]. Different dates from the
  Fishing Year chum row; left for the Fishing Year data to carry as its own row.
- Fall coho, other Puget Sound rivers: Snohomish closed to all fishing Oct 3 to Nov 1 [B];
  Stillaguamish closed Oct 9 to Nov 1 [B]; Skykomish salmon closed Nov 1 to 30 [B]. The Fishing
  Year row is the 2027 season; 2026 rules are not mapped onto it.
- Winter steelhead: no 2026-27 river list from WDFW yet (2025-26 list was by emergency rule).
  Hoh, Quillayute, Sol Duc, Bogachiel, Calawah, Dickey closed to all fishing from Oct 19 until
  further notice; Clearwater from Oct 16; upper Quinault from Oct 24 [B].
- Fall Chinook, Cowlitz: no fall Chinook retention in 2026 [A] (hatchery coho only Aug 1 to
  Dec 31). Olympic Peninsula rivers with a Chinook allowance are under the closures above.
