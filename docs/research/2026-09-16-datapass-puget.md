## UNSUPPORTED

- Areas 10 and 11 | Blackmouth | bestMonths [3]. [LOAF25] planned "3/1 – 4/30", but the 2025 opener was April 2 and the 2026-27 plan opens March 31, 2027. March is marginal at best; consider best [4], good [3].
- Area 7 | Halibut | goodMonths [4]: supported for 2026 (April 2 open, [HAL26]), but August/September reopening not reflected; consider good [4,8,9].
- Area 5 | Chinook | note "often odd days only": no source found; [LOAF25] 7/1 row has no day restriction.
- Area 9 | Market Squid | best [11,12,1], good [10,2]: [SQUID] says Edmonds starts "around September", so this may be late for Area 9. Not contradicted.
- Area 12 | Market Squid: no WDFW source mentions Hood Canal squid ([SQUID] does not reference Hood Canal).
- Area 13 | Market Squid best [11,12,1]: [SQUID] "throughout South Puget Sound in December and January" roughly supports; November is early.
- Area 5 | Lingcod note "Strait lingcod short spring opener": consistent with [WAC-LING]; halibut-day lingcod retention seaward of 120 ft (May 1-Jun 15) also exists. Fine.

## OMISSION (list only)

- Area 6: Port Angeles summer squid. [SQUID] "present at City Pier in Port Angeles ... from late June to the end of August."
- Area 7: summer hatchery Chinook (July), and spot shrimp ("Shrimping can be very productive during spring and early-summer openers", san-juan-islands page).
- Area 9: halibut ("typically open in April through June", admiralty-inlet page).
- Area 10: summer hatchery Chinook (Jul/Aug, [LOAF25] "7/17 – 8/31 ... release unmarked Chinook"); odd-year pinks.
- Area 11: summer hatchery Chinook (June and late July-Sept, [LOAF25]/[PROP26]); odd-year pinks.
- Area 12: hatchery Chinook south of Ayock Point Jul-Sep ([LOAF25] "7/1 – 9/30 South of Ayock Pt. ... Chinook 20" min size"); odd-year pinks.
- Areas 8-2, 9, 10, 11, 13: spot shrimp (short spring openers).
