Best times to go

  • Time of dayDawn and the last hour of light. One local coho report was a hookup at about 7 a.m.
  • Day of weekWeekdays. Weekend crowds on the Kent bank spots spook fish. The best single day is the one after a rain.
  • WaterRising or freshly raised water fishes best. Low, clear, shallow water with no rain is the slowest.
  • Coho tacticsLocal anglers call Green coho lockjawed. Go smaller on baits and lighter on leaders.
  • Chum tacticsNovember is the month. Anglers have called the Green chum bite fantastic, with the best days right after rain.

Rules for the lower Green

  • Salmon limit6 a day, no more than 3 adult coho and chum combined
  • Minimum size12 in
  • ChinookRelease  Retention is closed
  • S 212th StBelow the S 212th St bridge the season opened Aug 20. Above it, up to the Hwy 18 bridge, it opened Sept 16. Both are open now through Dec 31.
  • NovemberThe Green has had a night closure and anti-snagging rule in November before. Confirm for this year.

Rules change by emergency rule while the pamphlet stays the same, so read the WDFW emergency rules the morning you go. Sources: eRegulations, Green (Duwamish) special rules · WDFW rule #26-155 · Northwest Fishing Reports.
