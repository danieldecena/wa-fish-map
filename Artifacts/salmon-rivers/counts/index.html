How to read it

  • Anglers is bank anglers plus boat rods for that river and week, so it repeats on each species row. Bank and Boat rods are its two parts.
  • Kept/100 is fish kept per 100 anglers for that species. Blank when Kept or Anglers is blank.
  • A blank Kept or Released means the report did not state it. It is not zero.
  • Jacks are young males and have their own rows. Where the report says "jacks" without a species, the row says so.
  • Rivers split by reach (Cowlitz, Klickitat, Washougal) or section (Lower Columbia) show it in the River cell.
  • Rows are interviews at select access sites, not every angler. Click a row for the sentence it came from.
  • Week from says whether the week came from the report's file name or was worked out from its date.

Not in this table

Rivers outside the creel section of these reports are not in the database.
