Directions

Chinook are closed this year

An August emergency rule closed Chinook retention on the Green. The upper Green is also closed to all fishing Sept 16 to Oct 31.

Best times to go

Time of day
Dawn and the last hour of light. One local coho report was a hookup at about 7 a.m.
Day of week
Weekdays. Weekend crowds on the Kent bank spots spook fish. The best single day is the one after a rain.

Rules for the lower Green

Salmon limit
6 a day, no more than 3 adult coho and chum combined
Chinook
Retention is closed
