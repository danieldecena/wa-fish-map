Note

WDFW’s current order, effective Oct 18, allows 2 salmon a day from the mouth to the Marblemount Bridge, coho only, with no bait and single barbless hooks. It runs through Oct 31 unless WDFW extends it.

Before you go

Guidance, not regulation. Seasons change and can close with little notice. Confirm the rules on WDFW before you fish.
