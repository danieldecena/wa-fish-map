How to read it

  • Anglers is bank anglers plus boat rods for that river and week, so it repeats on each species row.
  • A blank Kept or Released means the report did not state it. It is not zero.
  • Jacks are young males and are shown on their own rows. Where the report says "jacks" without a species, the row says so.
  • Rivers split by reach (Cowlitz, Klickitat) or section (Lower Columbia) show the reach in the River cell.
  • Rows are interviews at select access sites, not every angler on the river.
